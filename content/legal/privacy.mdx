> **Plain-English summary.** YakRigged is a small editorial site. We don't
> sell your data, we don't run third-party ad networks, and we don't drop
> tracking cookies. The only personal data we hold is your email address if
> you subscribed to the newsletter, and we hand that to our newsletter
> provider (Beehiiv) — nowhere else. The longer version is below for the
> people who need it (and for regulators).

This page describes how YakRigged ("we", "us") handles information about
visitors to **yakrigged.com**. We try to keep it short and honest. If you
want the regulatory citations, see the [How this maps to
GDPR / CCPA](#how-this-maps-to-gdpr--ccpa) section at the end.

## What we collect

We only collect what's listed below. Anything not on this list, we don't
collect.

### 1. Newsletter email address

If you submit the **Subscribe** form (footer, article CTA, or `/subscribe`
page), we collect:

- The **email address** you typed.
- The **source** of the signup (e.g. `footer`, `article`, `/subscribe`) so
  we know which CTA actually works.
- Any **UTM parameters** present in the URL (`utm_source`, `utm_medium`,
  `utm_campaign`) — these tell us if the signup came from a specific
  marketing link.
- The **referring page** (your browser's `Referer` header) — same purpose.

That data is forwarded to our newsletter provider, **Beehiiv**, which
stores and sends the actual emails. We do not store the email address in
any other database on our side. Beehiiv's privacy practices are documented
at <https://beehiiv.com/privacy>.

You can unsubscribe at any time by clicking the **Unsubscribe** link at
the bottom of any newsletter email. When you unsubscribe, Beehiiv stops
emailing you immediately; their normal retention policy applies to the
record itself.

### 2. Anonymous traffic analytics

We use **Vercel Analytics** and **Vercel Speed Insights** to understand
which pages are popular and whether the site is fast.

These tools are **cookieless** and do not assign you a persistent
identifier. They count pageviews and aggregate performance metrics. They
do **not** build a profile of you across sessions or sites. Vercel's
documentation on this:
<https://vercel.com/docs/analytics/privacy-policy>.

### 3. Standard server logs

Like every web server on the public internet, our host (**Vercel**)
records basic request metadata when you visit a page:

- Your IP address (used to route the response and detect abuse).
- The page URL you requested.
- Your browser's `User-Agent` string.
- The timestamp.

These logs exist for operational and security reasons (debugging, abuse
prevention, rate-limiting). We do not use them for advertising or
profiling. Vercel's retention policy applies; we do not export or analyze
these logs in any other system.

## What we do NOT collect

For clarity, here's what we are **not** doing:

- ❌ No third-party advertising cookies (no Google Ads, no Meta Pixel, no
  ad network of any kind).
- ❌ No cross-site tracking pixels.
- ❌ No selling of your email or any other data to anyone.
- ❌ No first-party tracking cookies. The site does not set a cookie when
  you load a page.
- ❌ No "essential" cookie banner because there are no cookies to consent
  to. (If we ever add one, we will add a banner and update this page
  *before* it goes live.)

## Embedded videos (YouTube)

Some articles embed YouTube videos. We use the **`youtube-nocookie.com`**
privacy-enhanced domain, which means:

- Before you click **Play**, no Google cookies are set in your browser and
  no data is sent to Google about you.
- When you do click Play, the video is served by YouTube directly, and at
  that point Google's own privacy policy applies (they may set cookies and
  log the playback).

If you don't want Google to see the playback, just don't press Play. The
video thumbnail and player frame on their own don't tell Google anything
about you.

## Affiliate links (Amazon Associates)

YakRigged is a participant in the **Amazon Associates Program** and a few
similar affiliate networks. When we recommend gear, we sometimes link to
the retailer's product page using an affiliate URL. If you click that link
and buy something, the retailer pays us a small commission **at no extra
cost to you**.

A few things to know:

- Every page that contains affiliate links displays an **Affiliate
  Disclosure** at the top. We don't hide this.
- The destination retailer (e.g. Amazon) drops their own cookies once you
  land on their site so they can attribute the sale. Their privacy policy
  governs that — not ours.
- We do not see your purchase details. We only see aggregate commission
  reports from the retailer's affiliate dashboard.
- Editorial picks are made before the affiliate link is added. We don't
  rank gear by commission rate.

## Email correspondence

If you email us at <hello@yakrigged.com> (or reply to a newsletter), we
keep that email in our inbox so we can respond and refer back to the
conversation. We don't add your email to the newsletter list just because
you wrote to us — you have to actively subscribe through the form.

## Children's privacy

YakRigged is aimed at adults shopping for fishing gear. We do not
knowingly collect data from anyone under 13 (or under 16 in the EU). If
you believe a child has submitted their email to our newsletter, write to
<hello@yakrigged.com> and we'll delete the record.

## Your rights

You can, at any time:

- **See what we have on you.** Email <hello@yakrigged.com> and we'll send
  back whatever's on file (in practice: your newsletter email + signup
  metadata, or nothing if you haven't subscribed).
- **Delete it.** Same email address — we'll remove you from Beehiiv and
  confirm.
- **Unsubscribe.** Use the link at the bottom of any newsletter, no email
  required.
- **Object to processing.** We don't do automated decision-making or
  profiling, but if you have a specific concern, write to us and we'll
  address it.

We aim to respond within 7 days; the legal maximum (GDPR Art. 12) is 30.

## How this maps to GDPR / CCPA

Because the questions come up — here's the regulator-readable version.

- **Data controller:** YakRigged (contact via <hello@yakrigged.com>).
- **Lawful basis for the newsletter (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)):** your explicit
  consent, given by submitting the subscribe form.
- **Lawful basis for analytics and server logs (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)):**
  legitimate interest in running and securing the site, balanced against
  minimal data and no persistent identifier.
- **Categories of personal data processed:** email address, IP address,
  approximate technical metadata (browser, referrer, UTM).
- **Recipients / processors:** Beehiiv (newsletter delivery), Vercel
  (hosting + analytics).
- **International transfers:** Vercel and Beehiiv are US-based. Where you
  are in the EU/UK, transfers rely on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or
  equivalent Standard Contractual Clauses maintained by those processors.
- **Retention:**
  - Newsletter email — until you unsubscribe or request deletion.
  - Server logs — Vercel's default retention (typically 30 days).
  - Analytics — aggregated; no individual record to delete.
- **CCPA "Do Not Sell or Share":** we do not sell or share personal
  information as those terms are defined in CCPA. There is nothing to opt
  out of, but you may still email us to request deletion.

## Changes to this policy

When we materially change this policy, we'll update the **last-updated
date** at the top and — if the change affects existing subscribers — send
a notice in the next newsletter. Cosmetic / clarifying edits don't trigger
a notice.
