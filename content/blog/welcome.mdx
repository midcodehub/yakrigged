If you found this site, you probably tried to research kayak fishing gear
online recently and came away frustrated. Same. That's why we started
**YakRigged**.

<KeyTakeaway>
YakRigged is an independent kayak fishing gear publication. Every product we review gets a minimum of 10 hours on the water. We disclose all affiliate relationships upfront, and brands never preview reviews before publication.
</KeyTakeaway>

## What you can expect

- **Independent gear reviews** — every product tested by us on the water
- **Step-by-step rigging guides** — with photos of mistakes, not just the
  finished result
- **Destination notes** — short, honest write-ups of launches we've actually
  fished

## Cadence

We publish at least one long-form article per week, usually Tuesday morning
US Eastern. Subscribe via [RSS](/rss.xml) — we'll never email you because
we don't collect emails.
