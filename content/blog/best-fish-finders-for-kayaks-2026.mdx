  - q: How big a battery do I actually need for a kayak fish finder?
    a: For a 5-inch unit on a day trip, a 7Ah sealed lead-acid (SLA) battery is plenty. Switch to LiFePO4 if weight matters or you're running multiple electronics like a livewell pump and lights.
  - q: Is side-imaging worth it on a kayak?

    a: Yes — most modern kayaks ship with a flat accessory pad behind the seat. Use a RAM mount base with strong VHB tape there. The transducer can ride in a scupper hole instead of a through-hull install.

## Mounting tips that save you money

1. **Always use dielectric grease on the transducer pigtail.** Saltwater
   anglers — this single $4 tube has saved me two units.
2. **Don't run the head unit cable through the same conduit as your battery
   wires.** Even short runs will introduce noise on cheaper transducers.
3. **Test mount with painter's tape first.** Permanent holes in a $1,500 hull
   should never be a first-attempt decision.
